# Summary: NutrYWell Laboratories Business Case

## Company & Strategic Context
* **NutrYWell** is a rapidly growing European nutritional supplements company. 
* To expand into the sports nutrition segment, they recently **acquired the nutritional division** of a larger sports equipment group.
* They are currently in a **Transitional Service Agreement (TSA)**, meaning they temporarily rely on the former parent company's legacy logistics and distribution networks while integrating the new acquisition.

## Core Problem & Symptoms
* **Structural Complexity:** The acquisition introduced a dual distribution logic, separated systems, and limited end-to-end visibility.
* **Operational Strain:** The current Order-to-Delivery process is struggling to keep up with growth. While teams are working hard to resolve issues reactively, metrics like delivery reliability and planning stability are slipping.
* **Unsustainability:** The company relies too heavily on short-term fixes, manual adjustments, and "heroic" execution efforts, which is not scalable in the long run.

## The Project Challenge
* The TSA is ending soon, forcing NutrYWell to internalize or redesign its distribution model.
* The Executive Committee has commissioned a task force to diagnose the **Order-to-Delivery process**. 
* **Goal:** Determine the root causes of these operational tensions (are they isolated issues or systemic structural flaws?) and decide if the company can survive with incremental tweaks, or if it needs a fundamental redesign of its supply chain operating model before the transition period ends.
